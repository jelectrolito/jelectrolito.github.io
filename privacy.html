Privacy policy

This app is used only by its owner, to access their own Google Drive with rclone on their own computer.

  • Data read from Google Drive stays on the owner's computer. It is not sent to any other server, sold, shared or used for advertising.
  • The app collects no data about anyone else and has no other users.
  • Access can be removed at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
